Our Story

About Butter Story

A premium bakery and cafe chain in Lucknow, loved for its handcrafted bakes and warm hospitality.

Baked fresh. Served with warmth.

Butter Story is a premium bakery and cafe known for its handcrafted cakes and pastries. Every creation is made with fine ingredients and careful craftsmanship, so celebrations, mornings and coffee breaks all taste a little more special.

Step into any of our cafes and you will find elegant white interiors with warm golden lighting, a cozy cafe setting and plenty of seating — the perfect place to unwind with friends, family or a quiet cup of coffee.

● 100% Pure Vegetarian
